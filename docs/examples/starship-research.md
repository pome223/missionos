# What the spacecraft research supports

The model starts from public primary-source descriptions and explicitly marks
unidentified assumptions. Vehicle generations, planned operations, observed
events, and mechanism outcomes must not be combined into a single validated
Flight 14 reconstruction.

The [vehicle inventory](../agents/starship-vehicle-research.md),
[mission inventory](../agents/starship-mission-research.md), and
[6DOF source inventory](../agents/starship-sixdof-research.md) retain source
links and confidence boundaries. Visual measurements are documented separately
in the [visible-reference ledger](../agents/starship-visual-research.md).

The implemented development model couples rigid translation/rotation, finite
actuators, instantaneous mass properties, separation, and independently
propagated payload bodies. Its simplified plate aerodynamics, navigation truth,
and generic guidance are not identified SpaceX software or flight performance.
Numerical reference comparisons are limited to the stated subproblems.

The [showcase](starship-mission-showcase.md) shows what connected to MissionOS
and what remained negative. Launch-derived catch, robustness, qualified
capture/divert effects, and the complete operator catch workflow are unfinished.
Satellite-body release is not Starlink commissioning or communications service.
