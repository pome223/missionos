# Predeclared ANWM resident-runtime pilot (2026-09-26)

This follows the absolute-capability and single-SSH transport cohorts. Preserve
both cohorts, including every target miss. Rules superiority is not a criterion.

## Question and fixed scope

Can preloading the identical released ANWM checkpoint before camera capture bring
observation-to-selection **and dispatch** to at most **120 wall seconds in all
three** known static urban scenes? Initialization, GPU rental, and the wait for
readiness are reported separately; they are not eliminated costs.

One fresh gap, climb, and detour trial, in that order. Keep genuine 16-frame
RGB-D/pose input, the existing 5 m candidate pose prefixes, seed 42, 250 diffusion
steps, the pinned model/code/VAE, and the existing RGB goal-MSE selector. No
training, post-hoc score weights, scene-label heuristics, shortened sampling, or
LPIPS route selection. Do not attribute independent Rules intervention to WAM.

## Changed boundary

Use opt-in `resident_ssh_tar_v1`. Start the published runtime's sequential worker
before capturing observations. It verifies the upstream revision and checkpoint
SHA256 and loads the model once. Each fresh request is still validated and bound
to the same model paths and sampling steps. Per-candidate seeds and model calls
are unchanged. A single SSH stream carries exactly request.json/assets.npz and
returns the bound forecasts. No listening service, Jev key, approval record,
dispatch key, private source file, or future actual image is uploaded.

Record worker readiness with runtime/checkpoint digests, worker identity,
initialization and load durations. Model receipts retain actual call counts and
explicit resident reuse. This is a direct experimental runner, not a claim that
Gateway stop/reobserve uses WAM.

## Absolute endpoints and stop conditions

Keep the existing 180-wall-second observation ceiling and all independent safety
gates. Arrival is within 0.3 m with 1 sim second dwell, followed by observed
landing/disarm. Record building contact notifications (zero required), sampled
segment clearance (at least 0.25 m beyond a 0.6 m vehicle sphere), route length
(at most 60 m), and duration (at most 120 sim seconds). Report arrival, raw-choice
admissibility, requested maneuver, and Rules changes separately. The latency
engineering target is stricter than dispatch's safety ceiling.

Do not retry model failures, unfavorable routes, latency misses, or successful
flights. At most one infrastructure retry per scene is allowed only before any
prepared model input exists. Count all attempts. A failed/expired input cannot be
made fresh by reusing its files. No efficacy claim from three known scenes and
no comparison against an idealized Rules planner.

The worker handles at most three requests and admits them only during its first
2400 wall seconds, including initialization. An in-flight request may finish
after that admission deadline. The independent VM deadline is at most 2700
seconds with automatic VM and boot-disk deletion. Reserve at most USD 1.50 for
this cohort, enforce the retained cumulative USD 10 cap, and verify deletion.
If the owned resources or cost cannot be bounded, do not start another VM.
Shared quota waiting is at most 10 minutes, with no unrelated-resource changes.

## Admission and reporting

Before cloud execution: freeze this protocol's bytes, source hashes and commit;
run identity/lifetime/invalid-input contract tests, a two-request real subprocess
transport smoke with a clearly labeled fixture model, and the CLI validation
entrypoint. Publish the reviewed runtime before its authorized transfer.

Report the new cohort independently, even if it misses 120 seconds. Keep the
single-SSH cohort's slow results, the LPIPS diagnostic's failure to fix maneuver
selection, and the projection-support limitation. Better latency alone does not
establish reliable gap/climb selection, collision prediction, time-aligned
forecasting, dynamic-obstacle avoidance, or physical flight.
